x402-reputation

Did the merchant actually deliver? Reliability scores computed from signed fulfillment attestations

Paid routes

  • $0.001GET /score/{merchantId} — Buy a merchant's signed reliability report: score, fulfillment/refund/failure rates, attestor spread, a sample of recent attestations, and confidence
  • $0.001POST /attest — Record what happened after a payment settled

Free routes

  • GET /merchants — Every merchant with attestations on file, so you know which ids are worth scoring
  • GET /attestations/{id} — One signed attestation record, so a report's sample can be re-checked individually
  • POST /verify — Verify the HMAC-SHA256 signature of any attestation or score report
  • GET /health — Liveness probe

Pay in USDC on Base or Solana — your client picks the rail. An unpaid request answers 402 with both options and the schema of what you are buying; retry with an X-PAYMENT header and the artifact comes back in the same round trip.
